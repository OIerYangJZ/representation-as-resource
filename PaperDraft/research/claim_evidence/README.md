# Claim-evidence matrix

`claim_evidence.json` maps every headline abstract, introduction, resource, and
conclusion claim to one primary theorem, proposition, table, figure, or artifact
test plus its authorized scope. `claim_evidence_rows.tex` is the generated
reader-facing projection used in Appendix B.

The matrix is deliberately restrictive: configured-tool growth is diagnostic,
the checked file-backed trace is an accounting fixture rather than lower-bound
evidence, a revised restart-tracer campaign is required to test the formal
tradeoff empirically, and physical resource numbers are scoped to the disclosed
QRE model.
