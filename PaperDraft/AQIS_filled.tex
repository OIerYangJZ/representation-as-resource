\abstract{
Quantum circuit lowering is usually treated as semantics-preserving translation. We show that, for
resource-bounded compilation, lowering can destroy the recoverability of global diagonal-phase
aggregation. For Fourier-layer circuits of the form $H_\Lambda D_m(\theta)^rH_\Lambda$, compilers
below a diagonal-aggregation capability threshold retain $\Omega(rm)$ bounded-arity phase-gadget
atoms, while semantic-first aggregation emits $O(m)$ atoms. A converse classification shows that any
compiler achieving the bounded scaling must instantiate an equivalent global representation. Fixed-width
experiments validate the separation against Qiskit, PyZX, and TKET baselines, and quantify the resulting
fault-tolerant resource-estimation inflation.
}

\keywords{quantum compilation, circuit lowering, QFT, phase-polynomial optimization, semantic IR}

\section{Motivation and Context}

Quantum circuit lowering---the projection of high-level structure into a fixed hardware-native
basis---is conventionally treated as a semantics-preserving translation. This view conflates unitary
equivalence with structural conservation. Lowering preserves the unitary, but it need not preserve
the \emph{recoverability} of an optimization opportunity for a resource-bounded compiler. Before
lowering, a commuting diagonal phase layer names its generators and coefficients explicitly, so
repeated layers aggregate by adding coefficients. After lowering, the same information is spread
across many locally similar fragments, and a compiler limited in passes, scanning window, working
memory, and persistent metadata faces an information bottleneck: it cannot recognize the fragments
as members of one global aggregate without spending enough summary or lifting resource.

This phenomenon occurs in Fourier-based subroutines---QFT, AQFT, QPE, and amplitude estimation---which
naturally contain commuting diagonal phase layers
$D_m(\theta)=\prod_{j=1}^{m}\exp(-i\theta_jP_j/2)$ sandwiched between Hadamard or Fourier
basis-change boundaries. We formalize the resulting boundary, prove that it is an asymptotic
separation rather than a pass-ordering artifact, and validate it experimentally.

\section{Main Contributions}

\paragraph{Resource-bounded recoverability.}
We introduce a six-parameter capability class $\mathrm{RB}(p,w,s,b,q,\lambda)$ over passes $p$,
sliding-window radius $w$, ephemeral state $s$, persistent metadata $b$, lifted rewrites $q$, and
total lifted volume $\lambda$, yielding a hierarchy
$\mathrm{LF}\subseteq\mathrm{SF}\subseteq\mathrm{SAF}\subseteq\mathrm{HYB}\subseteq\mathrm{SEM}$ from
local flat compilers up to fully semantic ones. Within it we isolate a witness-specific capability,
diagonal aggregation $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}$: a compiler instantiates $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}$
on $C_{m,r}$ if, at some point in execution, it constructs data sufficient to associate each lowered
occurrence with its semantic role across repetitions, represent the aggregate coefficient
$r\theta_j\bmod 2\pi$, and justify replacing the $r$ repetitions of each non-removable $P_j$ by
$O(1)$ representatives. This is a capability condition, not a budget region.

\paragraph{Fourier-layer separation theorem.}
For the non-inverse family $C_{m,r}:=H_\Lambda D_m(\theta)^{r}H_\Lambda$:

\begin{theorem}[Fourier-layer recoverability separation]\label{thm:sep}
Fix constants $p,w,s,b,q,\lambda$ and a bounded-arity lowering scheme. Under bounded arity of the
$P_j$, non-resonance ($r\theta_j\not\equiv 0\bmod 2\pi$), and periodic separated lowering, every
compiler below the threshold, $A\in\mathrm{RB}^{<\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}}$, satisfies
\[
\mathrm{pg}_B\!\big(A(L_B(C_{m,r}))\big)\ \ge\ c\,rm - O(m),
\]
while a semantic compiler $S$ that aggregates coefficients before lowering emits
$\mathrm{pg}_B(L_B(S(C_{m,r})))\le C\,m$ for absolute constants $c,C>0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Fixed-width form]\label{cor:fixed}
At fixed width $m=m_0>w$, $C_{m_0,r}$ separates the two classes as $\Omega(r)$ versus $O(1)$. For the
canonical $n=4$ witness, the constant output decomposes deterministically as
$\underbrace{4\!\cdot\!1}_{\mathrm{RZ}}+\underbrace{6\!\cdot\!5}_{\mathrm{CP}\to\mathrm{cx,rz}}
+\underbrace{8}_{\mathrm{boundary\ H}}=42$ gates.
\end{corollary}

An analogous separation holds for a second family built from conjugation and mirrored/self-inverse
shells, $G_r=P\circ C^{r}\circ S$, where the obstruction is again local-profile multiplicity that
bounded compilers cannot collapse without paying a global summary; this places Grover-like repeated
shells in the same classification as the Fourier-layer witness.

\paragraph{Necessity theorem.}

\begin{theorem}[Necessity of the aggregation resource]\label{thm:nec}
Assume the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:sep} and that the generators $\{P_j\}$ are independent as
diagonal characters. Any semantics-preserving compiler $K$---with no restriction on its internal
algorithm---that emits a target-basis circuit with $\mathrm{pg}_B(K(C_{m,r}))=O(m)$ for unbounded $r$
must instantiate $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}$: it must preserve, summarize, lift, or reconstruct an
equivalent global diagonal representation.
\end{theorem}

This is a classification, not a universal impossibility: a future flat-looking compiler that recovers
the bounded form is thereby shown to have funneled resource into one of these channels---a global
phase-polynomial, ZX, stabilizer-phase, or commuting-diagonal IR, or an equivalent algebraic rewrite.

\paragraph{Tool-independent validation.}
We realize the resource-rich side two ways: a bounded semantic-UCC artifact over a term algebra
$T ::= \mathrm{Prim}\mid T_1\!\circ\!T_2\mid T^r\mid \mathrm{Conj}\mid \mathrm{Fourier}\mid
\mathrm{Mirror}$ with late materialization, and a stripped-down phase-polynomial reference that only
aggregates the commuting \texttt{rz}/\texttt{cp} coefficients. On the $n=4$ fixed-width witness both
return a constant 42-gate, depth-24, 12-\texttt{cx} circuit from 4k to 100k input gates, while
\texttt{qiskit opt3}, a PyZX bridge, and TKET FullPeephole grow or time out
(Table~\ref{tab:headline}). External bridges concur in failure: at requested 10k inputs on the
full-pair topology, PyZX emits 33{,}974 gates and TKET times out, against the same 42-gate semantic
output. A width sweep gives 42, 65, and 93 gates for $n=4,5,6$, confirming the $O(m)$ law with
$m=n(n-1)/2$; all 45 configurations are certified by exact unitary equivalence with zero variance
across nonresonant phase seeds.

\section{Techniques}

The lower bound rests on a local-profile multiplicity lemma.

\begin{lemma}[Local-profile multiplicity]\label{lem:profile}
Let $C_r$ be a repeated lowered family with $\Theta(r)$ interior semantic positions whose radius-$w$
neighborhoods of interior copies of the same semantic role are identical up to translation outside an
$O(1)$ prefix/suffix anomaly zone. For any compiler $A\in\mathrm{RB}^{<\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}}$,
the accessible profile of an interior position---the union of visible local windows, bounded pass
state, persistent summaries, and lifted regions---has multiplicity $\Theta(r)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The local-window assumption gives $\Theta(r)$ translated copies with identical radius-$w$ evidence;
bounded pass state can only process that evidence through the same finite local interface on each
copy. If $A\in\mathrm{RB}^{<\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}}$, then by definition its execution does not
construct, preserve, or reconstruct the association between repeated lowered occurrences and semantic
roles. Therefore its accessible profile does not distinguish interior copies as members of a global
aggregate class. Collapsing a constant fraction of those copies into $O(1)$ representatives requires
building the role-to-occurrence map and the aggregate coefficient---that is, instantiating
$\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}$.
\end{proof}

The upper bound in Theorem~\ref{thm:sep} is the Abelian aggregation identity
$\big(\prod_j e^{-i\theta_jP_j/2}\big)^r=\prod_j e^{-i(r\theta_j)P_j/2}$. The necessity theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:nec}) runs it in reverse: since $K$ is semantics-preserving, conjugating its output
by the Hadamard boundary leaves the same diagonal unitary, and independence of the characters fixes
$(r\theta_1,\dots,r\theta_m)\bmod 2\pi$ up to global phase. An $O(m)$ output must therefore carry
$O(1)$ information per term encoding $r\theta_j$, which is precisely aggregation-equivalent recovery.

A Fourier-path ablation isolates causality: disabling the semantic IR forces timeout at every scale,
while Fourier-enabled paths deterministically recover the 42-gate form. Minimal pass-reordering
repairs to the default UCC pipeline fail to close the gap; on the official
\texttt{phase\_estimation\_real} instance the strongest minimal-bundle repair returns 485{,}780 gates
against 166{,}805 for the semantic compiler and \texttt{qiskit opt3}, ruling out a simple
pass-ordering explanation.

\section{Significance}

\paragraph{Fault-tolerant resource estimation.}
Using the Ross--Selinger synthesis scaling $T_\epsilon(R)=\lceil 3\log_2(1/\epsilon)\rceil R$ as a
proxy, semantic-first compilation holds the fixed-width witness at 22 rotations and a constant
$T_{10^{-10}}$ proxy of 2{,}200, whereas materialize-first \texttt{qiskit opt3} reaches a proxy of
$2.4\!\times\!10^{6}$ at 20k before timing out (Table~\ref{tab:resource}). Delayed aggregation
inflates the rotation multiplicity feeding magic-state and T-count estimates---an $\Omega(r)$ effect
on downstream distillation demand.

\paragraph{Architecture.}
The result reclassifies semantic term layers, projected-cost selection, and delayed materialization
from arbitrary engineering choices into the mechanism by which a compiler crosses the threshold. The
boundary is falsifiable: a pipeline recovering the bounded form without any equivalent global
representation would break the threshold, while recovery via global reconstruction confirms the
classification. The minimal $HD^rH$ witness strips away routing and approximation noise, serving as a
precise probe of this representation boundary. Anti-regression behaviour on real QFT, QPE, and QAOA
instances---matching strong presets at 65--78\% baseline reduction---confirms the mechanism scales
beyond the minimal construction.

\section{Relation to Prior Work}

The separation isolates non-inverse, non-local Abelian phase aggregation, distinguishing it from
local $UU^\dagger$ inverse-cancellation frameworks that motivated much earlier circuit-optimization
work. It also offers a common lens on several advanced compilers. Phase-polynomial optimization by
Nam et al. builds an aggregate diagonal object before re-synthesis, instantiating
$\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}$ explicitly. ZX-calculus rewriting by Duncan et al. and PyZX operates on
a global semantic representation; the necessity theorem predicts that any ZX configuration achieving
the bounded form does so through this global object, and our PyZX-bridge experiments are consistent
with this prediction (the bridged pipeline does not recover the bounded form, because the bridge
itself flattens before ZX rewriting). Lazy synthesis by Martiel and Goubault de Brugi\`ere defers
premature lowering and thereby preserves the resource through a different channel. The necessity
theorem unifies these as different routes to instantiating $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{diag}}$ rather than
unrelated tools.

Unlike full-stack benchmarks such as MQT Bench and SupermarQ, $HD^rH$ is an isolated theorem witness
targeting the commuting-diagonal topology native to QFT, AQFT, and QPE, without conflating the
result with routing constraints or approximate-synthesis noise. A full technical version with all
definitions, proofs, and the complete experimental suite is provided as an appendix.
